# Acme Corp — Annual Leave & Time Off Policy

**Document ID**: POL-HR-001  
**Version**: 2026.1  
**Effective Date**: January 1, 2026  
**Owner**: Human Resources Department  
**Access Level**: PUBLIC_INTERNAL (All Full-Time Employees)  

---

## 1. Overview and Purpose
Acme Corp recognizes the importance of work-life balance and provides eligible employees with paid time off for vacation, personal affairs, illness, and bereavement.

---

## 2. Annual Leave Entitlements
Full-time employees accrue paid time off based on their employment status and location:

### 2.1 Privilege Leave (PL) / Earned Leave
- All full-time employees receive **21 business days** of Privilege Leave per calendar year.
- PL accrues at a rate of 1.75 days per completed month of service.
- Employees may carry forward up to **10 days** of unused PL into the following calendar year. Unused leave beyond 10 days will lapse on December 31st.

### 2.2 Casual Leave (CL)
- Full-time employees receive **8 business days** of Casual Leave per calendar year.
- Casual Leave is intended for short-term personal needs or sudden emergencies.
- Unused Casual Leave cannot be carried forward to the next calendar year and cannot be encashed.

### 2.3 Sick Leave (SL)
- Full-time employees receive **10 business days** of Sick Leave per calendar year.
- A medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner is mandatory for sick leave exceeding **2 consecutive business days**.

---

## 3. Special Leave Categories

### 3.1 Parental & Maternity Leave
- Female employees are eligible for **26 consecutive weeks** of paid Maternity Leave for up to two surviving children.
- Primary caregivers adopting a child under 1 year of age are entitled to 12 weeks of paid leave.
- Male employees and secondary caregivers are entitled to **10 business days** of paid Paternity Leave, to be taken within 6 months of the child's birth or adoption.

### 3.2 Bereavement Leave
- Employees receive up to **5 consecutive business days** of paid leave in the event of the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling).

---

## 4. Leave Application & Approval Process
1. All leave requests must be submitted through the Acme HR Portal at least **3 business days** prior to the planned start date (except for emergency Casual or Sick Leave).
2. Leave approval rests with the employee's direct Reporting Manager.
3. The chatbot or automated AI assistant **cannot approve or modify leave requests**. All official leave submissions must go through the HR Portal.
