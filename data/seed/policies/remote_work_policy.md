# Acme Corp — Remote & Hybrid Work Policy

**Document ID**: POL-HR-002  
**Version**: 2026.1  
**Effective Date**: February 15, 2026  
**Owner**: Human Resources & IT Security  
**Access Level**: PUBLIC_INTERNAL (All Employees)  

---

## 1. Work Models
Acme Corp operates a hybrid work model for eligible positions:
- **Hybrid Employees**: Expected to work at least **2 days per week** from an designated Acme Corp office location.
- **Full Remote Employees**: Requires prior written approval from the Department Vice President and HR Business Partner.

---

## 2. Remote Work Stipend & Equipment
- Eligible full-time hybrid and remote employees receive a **one-time technology stipend of $500** upon onboarding to setup an ergonomic home workspace.
- Acme IT provides a corporate laptop, monitor, and encrypted security key. Personal computers are strictly prohibited for accessing production environments or customer data.
- Employees receive a monthly internet allowance of **$50/month**, processed via monthly payroll.

---

## 3. Security & Compliance Requirements when Working Remotely
1. Employees must connect via the Acme Corporate VPN (Zscaler/GlobalProtect) at all times when accessing internal systems.
2. Working from public Wi-Fi networks (e.g., coffee shops, airports) without an active corporate VPN is strictly prohibited.
3. Automated assistants and AI tools cannot grant remote work exception approvals. All remote work location changes must be filed via the HR Portal.
